## Project Overview

This quote covers two pieces of work at the clinic. The first moves your phones off the analog lines onto a business VoIP system, keeping both of your existing numbers and removing the limits that came with having only two lines. The second replaces the ISP router with a managed network that covers the whole office properly, so the far end of the building has the same coverage as the front.

Both of your numbers come with you. Every phone in the office rings on both, everything lands in one voicemail box rather than two, and every outbound call shows your main number. The new system is not built around lines at all, so the ceiling on how many calls you can handle at once disappears completely.

## Network Infrastructure

The clinic currently runs on the router supplied by your internet provider, which is why coverage drops off toward the back of the building. This replaces it with a managed system: a gateway that handles routing and security, a managed switch at the equipment location, and two access points sized and placed to cover the full floor plan.

The gateway also brings intrusion detection and prevention, which watches traffic for known threats and blocks them before they reach a workstation. For a practice holding patient records that is worth having, and it runs quietly in the background with nothing for staff to manage.

Your existing static IP address carries straight onto the new gateway, so the software that depends on it keeps working exactly as it does today.

## Additional Notes

**Your Existing Numbers:** Both 705-457-9500 and 705-457-9488 transfer to the new system and both stay fully active. Every phone in the office rings on either number, messages collect in a single voicemail box rather than one per line, and outbound calls display your main number. You remain live on your current service throughout the transfer, and we only switch over once the new system is tested. To file the transfer we will need a recent copy of your bill from your current provider, since the paperwork has to match the account exactly as it appears there.

**Fax Line:** Your fax stays exactly where it is on its existing line and is not part of this changeover. Faxing over an internet phone system has failure points outside anyone's control, and for prescriptions and clinical correspondence that risk is not worth taking. Leaving the fax on its own dedicated line is the reliable answer.

**Static IP Address:** Your existing static IP address moves onto the new gateway at no additional cost, so nothing that depends on it needs to change. If you later move to a different internet provider, the address will be a new one issued by them, and it needs to be requested at the time the service is ordered rather than added afterward.

**Desk Phone Selection:** The quote is priced on the T85W. The other three options change the package total to the figures shown with them. Note that the T88W Pro takes the total above the $5,000 mark, which moves the terms to 50% on approval and 50% on completion.

**Monthly Phone Service:** Your phone service is $64.98 per month plus HST. That is a single subscription covering the phone system, both of your numbers and one user seat, and it arrives as one line on one monthly invoice. There is no charge per phone. It is billed on its own monthly cycle and is not included in the totals above.

**How Many Simultaneous Calls:** There is no limit, inbound or outbound. The two call ceiling you live with today is a limitation of the analog lines, not of your numbers, and it does not carry over. Both numbers can be busy, and every phone can be on a call, all at the same time.

**User Seats:** All four phones are set up on a single seat, which matches how the practice works today. Every phone rings on both numbers, any phone can answer, and messages collect in one voicemail box. Nothing about that changes as you add phones, because phones themselves carry no monthly charge.

A second seat would give one position its own extension. Another phone in the office could then dial that desk directly, page it, or transfer a call straight to it, and a call to that extension that goes unanswered would land in that person's own voicemail rather than the shared box. That suits an office where individuals need to be reached by name, so it is worth considering if you would like the exam rooms reachable separately from the front desk. Seats are $24.99 per month each and can be added at any time without changing any hardware.

Once there is more than one seat, you also have the option of a recorded greeting on your main number, the kind that invites a caller to dial an extension for the person or department they want. That would mean callers hear a short recording rather than the phones ringing straight through, so we have not included it here, but it is available whenever you decide you want it.

**Power Supplies:** Yealink supplies these phones without a power adapter, so one is included per phone in the pricing above.

**Warranty:** Phone and network hardware carry their manufacturer warranties, and our installation and workmanship are covered for one full year.

**Scheduling:** Once the quote is approved we order the equipment, begin the number transfer, and schedule the installation date with you.
